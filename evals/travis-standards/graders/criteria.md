A successful response:
- Says the binding law is ADA Title II (the US Department of Justice rule for state and local governments).
- Names WCAG 2.1 Level AA as the standard.
- Gives the deadline for governments serving 50,000+ people as April 26, 2027 (the date after the
  April 2026 extension), not April 24, 2026.
- Says the Texas state accessibility rule does not bind a county (it covers state agencies and
  public universities), or otherwise does not claim the Texas rule is what the county is graded against.
- Does not call the site compliant and does not claim to have scanned it.
Fail if the answer grades the county against Section 508 or the Texas state rule as its main standard.
